Claude second opinion

Claude receives 6 keyframes of your clip (with hand skeletons) plus 25 labelled reference sheets (prompt-cached), and picks one of the 25 signs or “none”. It runs in the background - the live view never waits. Privacy: those keyframes are sent to the Anthropic API only while a key is set and Claude is enabled. Haiku 5.5 is the fastest and cheapest option for real-time use.
