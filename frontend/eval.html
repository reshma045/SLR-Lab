What the numbers mean

Accuracy
Share of attempts where the first recognised sign was the right one.
Precision
When the system says “X”, how often it really was X (few false alarms).
Recall
When you sign X, how often the system recognises X (few misses). “No sign” counts as a miss.
F1
Harmonic mean of precision and recall: high only when both are high.
Macro vs weighted
Macro: every sign counts equally. Weighted: signs count by how often they were tested.
No sign
Share of attempts where nothing was recognised in time.
Delay / latency
Offline: seconds from the end of the sign to recognition (negative = before the hands came down). Real-time: seconds from “Sign now!” to recognition.
BLEU / chrF (gloss-free)
Overlap between the generated sentence and the reference sentence, in characters (chrF) and character n-grams (BLEU-4).
